## CC0 textures (record only, nothing downloaded)
No CC0 library (ambientCG, Poly Haven, cgbookcase, Share Textures) has a true cellular-glass or pumice-cell texture. Build the cut face procedurally (see the report). Partial helper:
- ambientCG **Styrofoam004** (or Styrofoam002): fine closed-cell foam with normal, roughness and displacement maps, CC0. Recolour to charcoal, scale the cells up 3–5x, and add a sparkle/glint term. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Styrofoam004 (I saw only the preview sphere.)
- Checked and **not** suitable: Poly Haven `volcanic_rock_tiles` (a mosaic of tiles, not porous rock), `coral_ground_02`, `chipped_concrete`, `white_stucco`, and ambientCG `Lava00x`.
