Collected 2026-10-10. Every file was checked with `file` and opened to confirm what it shows.
The license labels are my reading of each source page. Check them again before shipping any image or derivative in the product.
I could not fetch armacell.com directly (HTTP 403 to scripted clients), so there are no Armacell first-party images. The K-Flex images stand in for the material class.

## Looked at and rejected
- Commons `Caj8073_armaflex_needs_intumescent.jpg` (public domain): fire-tested and charred, with text overlaid on the image.
- MTA "Installation of pipe insulation in the chiller room" (CC BY 2.0): fiberglass and other jackets, not FEF.

## CC0 textures (record only, nothing downloaded; I saw only the preview spheres)
- ambientCG **Leather037** (or Leather030): fine pebble grain. Desaturate it to black and tile it at roughly 0.5 mm per pebble as the **skin micro-normal**. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leather037
- ambientCG **Rubber004**: fine-grain black rubber for skin roughness variation. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rubber004
- ambientCG **Styrofoam004**: fine closed-cell normal and AO for the **cut face**. Recolour to near-black. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Styrofoam004
- Avoid ambientCG Rubber001 (speckled gym flooring).
