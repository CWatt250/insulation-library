## Construction details with numbers

Sources: the JM *Metal Jacketing Installation Guide* (link in `../jacket-aluminum/SOURCES.md`) and the JM Strapping & Seals data sheet.

**Material**
- T304 or T304L (standard), or T316 or T316L (corrosive environments), annealed, per **ASTM C1767 / A240**.
- Factory heat-laminated **3-mil polyfilm** inside.
- Roll and sheet are Type I, **0.010–0.024 in**. North American insulators call the standard pipe jacket "**oh-one-oh stainless**", which is **0.010 in (0.25 mm)**.

**Minimum thickness vs insulation OD** (JM Table 2)

| Insulation OD | Minimum thickness |
|---|---|
| ≤ 8 in | 0.010 in |
| > 8–11 in | 0.010 in |
| > 11–24 in | 0.010 in |
| > 24–36 in | 0.016 in |
| > 36 in | 0.020 in |

**Finishes and laps**
- Finishes are smooth (2B mill), plus 3/16 in corrugated and deep corrugated (1¼ in × ¼ in) sheets.
- **Laps and seam orientation are the same as aluminum**:
  - Longitudinal lap ≥ 2 in for OD < 16 in, ≥ 3 in for OD ≥ 16 in.
  - Circumferential lap ≥ 2 in.
  - On horizontal pipe the seam sits at 3–4 or 8–9 o'clock with the opening facing down. On vertical pipe the upper piece overlaps the lower.
- **Butt straps** are T304, **0.010 in × 2 in**, sold in 100 ft rolls.

**Bands and seals** (the same hardware is used on aluminum jacket)
- Stainless jacket and stainless elbows always use **stainless bands**: T304 annealed, **0.020 in thick**, **½ in wide (OD < 16 in) or ¾ in (OD ≥ 16 in)**. 0.015 in is acceptable on small, non-rigid insulation.
- Bands are **centered over each circumferential joint** plus **intermediates at ≤ 9 in o.c.** (≈ 11–12 in o.c. in JM's own 36 in-section example).
- Bands are tensioned with a pistol-grip "pusher bar" tensioner (MIP 1800), or a windlass tensioner for ¾ in.
- **Wing seal**: **0.032 in** T304 or T316, the same width as the band. It is a short sleeve, about 1 in long (my estimate), crimped around the doubled band end. Its two side "wings" fold down over the band, and the band tail (about 1 in) folds back over the seal. In profile it is about 2–2.5 mm proud of the jacket and the band is 0.5 mm proud.
- **Closed seals**: 1¼ in long, 0.024 in hard SS. They are used on vessels ≥ 8 ft dia and with expansion springs.
- **Fab straps**: pre-cut bands with the wing seal clinched on, sized to OD.

**Elbows**
- Two-piece pressed **T316, 0.016 in** covers are used where available. They have a pleated throat and are banded between the fingers on cold work.
- Otherwise elbows are **field-gored**. Hot work uses #8 × ½ in SS screws at about 3 in o.c. on the elbow seams and ≤ 6 in o.c. on the straight longitudinal seams.

## Visual / PBR guidance (sRGB)

- **2B mill finish jacket.** Base **`#C4C6C8`** (stainless F0 is about linear 0.55–0.56), metallic 1, **roughness 0.25–0.35**. It is milky and cloudy, with no strong grain. It reads slightly darker and warmer-grey than aluminum, and reflections are sharper and longer.
- **No. 4 brushed** (brewery cladding, some jacket). Same base color, roughness 0.18–0.28 with **anisotropy 0.6–0.8** aligned to the brushing direction, which is usually circumferential on vessels.
- **Bands.** Bright annealed SS, roughness 0.15–0.25. They catch a crisp specular line.
- Light heat tint or grime near seams is optional.
