## Notes
- No usable photo of an installed, **unjacketed** calsil elbow on a real line was found. In the field these are almost always hidden under gored aluminum or stainless elbow covers. The construction is shown by the fabricated-fitting photo and the JM mitered-fitting literature (Thermo-12 Gold Mitered Fittings data sheet).
- Rejected: Promat (Promatect) fire boards (a different, denser product), aluminum gored elbow-cover product shots (jacketing, not calsil), and probable renders or AI images from some overseas supplier sites.
- Distribution International's calsil elbow product photos (shop.distributioninternational.com/ASSETS/IMAGES/ITEMS/ZOOM/PIC6-*.jpg) are behind a Cloudflare challenge and could not be fetched with curl.
