## Looked at and rejected
- pipelagging.com, buyinsulationonline and insulation-more product images: mostly CGI renders of the same pink/foil section.
- A warehouse photo of a Kooltherm box with one section (buyinsulationonline): low detail.
- I found no CC-licensed photo of an installed phenolic pipe on Commons or Flickr.

## CC0 textures (record only, nothing downloaded; I saw only the preview spheres)
- ambientCG **Foil003** (smoother) / **Foil001** (more crinkled): crinkled aluminium foil normal and roughness maps for the **facing**. Add a procedural scrim grid. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foil003
- ambientCG **Styrofoam004**: very fine closed-cell normal and AO. Tint it salmon (#E99A82) for the **cut face**. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Styrofoam004
- ambientCG **Cork001**: pinkish porous surface. Its cells are too coarse for phenolic, so use it only as a colour-variation reference.
