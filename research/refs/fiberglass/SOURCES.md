## CC0 / CC-BY texture-library candidates (not downloaded; previews checked 2026-10-10)
No CC0 library checked (ambientCG, Poly Haven, cgbookcase) has an actual fiberglass scan. The closest proxies, all CC0:
- ambientCG **Fabric034** (grey/white felt): the best fibrous base for the cut face; tint it yellow. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric034
- Poly Haven **polar_fleece** (beige fuzzy fleece): fuzzy fibre normal/roughness. https://polyhaven.com/a/polar_fleece
- ambientCG **Paper001** (white paper): ASJ kraft base. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001
- ambientCG **Foil001** / **Foil003** (aluminium foil): FSK and ASJ+ foil facings. https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foil001 , https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foil003
- Photo-sourced: `macro-glass-wool-batt-01.jpg` above (CC0).
textures.com: the search UI is JS- and login-gated, so I could not confirm any glass-wool asset.
